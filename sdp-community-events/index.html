Juan C. Vera Degree Bounds for Polynomial Optimization Hierarchies: A Lagrangian Reformulation Approach
Abstract

Drawing upon strong duality results for non-convex problems, we recast polynomial optimization problems (POPs) over general compact semialgebraic sets into equivalent POPs over the hypercube. This approach extends established results for POPs over the hypercube to a broader class of POPs over compact semialgebraic sets. In particular, the analysis focuses on degree bounds for several sum-of-squares (SOS) hierarchies.

This talk is based on the works arXiv 2410.24111 and arXiv 2605.15821

Lucas Slot P-sätze and Prompts
Abstract

A substantial literature is devoted to proving error bounds for the moment-SOS hierarchy (or, equivalently, degree bounds for various Positivstellensätze). Recently, optimal bounds were obtained in two or three of the most studied settings by various (groups of) people prompting various AI models. These improve upon the state-of-the-art by a lot, and essentially close a line of research going back about two decades. I talk about an experimental, ongoing collaboration aimed at collecting these results and making their proofs accessible to human readers.
